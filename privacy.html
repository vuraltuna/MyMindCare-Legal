Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MyMindCare handles information when you use the iPhone app. MyMindCare is designed as a privacy-first mental wellness support app. It does not provide clinical diagnosis, medical treatment, medication advice or emergency care.

Information You May Provide

  • Profile information such as your first name, selected language, goals and notification preferences.
  • Mood check-ins, emotions, stress, anxiety, energy, sleep quality, triggers, notes and tags.
  • Journal entries and therapy-preparation summaries you create in the app.
  • Self-care habit logs, breathing sessions, sleep entries and SOS or panic-event records.
  • AI prompts and recent wellness context sent to the backend when you use AI Companion features.
  • Subscription status and purchase entitlement information provided by Apple StoreKit.

How Information Is Used

  • To show your mood, habit, sleep and journal history inside the app.
  • To generate supportive, non-clinical AI replies and personal insights when you choose to use AI features.
  • To create therapy-ready summaries based on your own records.
  • To manage Premium access, monthly AI usage limits and subscription-related app behavior.
  • To provide safety-aware crisis routing, including reminders that emergency support is never paywalled.

AI Processing

AI Companion requests are sent through Firebase Cloud Functions. The OpenAI API key is stored on the backend and is not shipped inside the iPhone app. When you ask for AI support, MyMindCare may send your message and limited recent context such as mood, sleep, habits, triggers and a short journal summary to the backend so a supportive reply can be generated.

AI replies are supportive and informational only. They do not diagnose, treat, prescribe medication or replace professional care. If self-harm or crisis signals appear, normal coaching should stop and the app should direct you toward emergency or trusted-person support.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple StoreKit, for subscription purchases and entitlement status.
  • Firebase and Google Cloud, for backend AI proxy functions, anonymous authentication and AI usage quota records.
  • OpenAI, for generating AI Companion responses when you use AI features.

MyMindCare does not sell your personal information and is not designed to track you for third-party advertising.

Local Storage, Export and Deletion

MyMindCare stores wellness records locally in the app by default. You can use privacy lock where available, export your data and delete your records from the app’s privacy controls. If you delete your data, local MyMindCare records are removed from the app.

Children and Sensitive Information

Mental wellness data can be sensitive. MyMindCare should be used only where permitted by local law and App Store age requirements. If you are a minor, use the app with guidance from a parent, guardian or appropriate professional support.

Emergency Disclaimer

MyMindCare is not an emergency service. If you may harm yourself or someone else, or if you are in immediate danger, call local emergency services or a local crisis hotline immediately.
